# Contact Vestia support

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This document is translated into several languages. In the event of any discrepancy between the versions, the French version shall prevail.

## Write to us

Write to us at: **contactervestia@gmail.com**

So that we can help you quickly, please include if possible:

- the email address of your Vestia account;
- your phone model and the app version (Profile, Help & legal, Version);
- a description of the problem, with a screenshot if it may help.

## Response time

We generally reply within 3 working days. This time frame is indicative.

For requests concerning your personal data (access, rectification, erasure, objection), we reply within one month at most, as required by the GDPR.

## Languages

We reply in French and English. Your messages are accepted in any language.

## Before writing to us

- Download your data: Profile, My data, "Download my data".
- Delete your account: Profile, "Delete my account".
- Read the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use: Profile, Help & legal.
