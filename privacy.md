# Vestia Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Vestia is a digital wardrobe: you keep your clothes in it, see what your wardrobe is worth and receive an outfit suggestion each day. This policy explains which data we use, why, who it is shared with and what your rights are. It complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## 1. Data controller

The data controller is Amine Berdai, a private individual and the publisher of the Vestia app, based in Portugal.

Contact: contactervestia@gmail.com

## 2. The data we use

### Your account

- Your email address, so that you can sign in (a sign-in link is sent by email).
- The technical identifier of your account.
- Your first name, if you choose to provide it.

### Your wardrobe

- Photos of your clothes: front photo, optional back photo, the original photo we keep, and the version on a white background.
- Details of each item: type, category, name, brand, colours, shade, purchase price, currency, the set it belongs to, and the dates it was added and removed.
- Automatic translations of the names and shades of your items, and any translations you correct yourself.
- Your outfit journal: the days and the items you wore.

### Your preferences

- Your style (style profile, favourite style), your budget, your display currency, the app language and your shopping country.
- Your settings: morning reminder, and whether your wardrobe's value may be shown publicly (this setting is not used by any feature at present: your wardrobe is shown to no one).

### Your location, for the weather only

If you allow it, the app reads your phone's location to show today's weather. It is rounded to about one kilometre and sent directly from your phone to the Open-Meteo weather service. The name of the city is found by Apple's location service built into the iPhone. **The location is never stored on your account.** You may type a city instead: its name is then sent to Open-Meteo to find its coordinates.

### Technical data

- The number of artificial intelligence requests made by your account and their cost, so that we can control expenses and prevent abuse.
- Our host's technical logs (IP address, date, function called), for security and to fix errors.

### What we do not do

- No advertising, no analytics tools, no tracking across apps or websites.
- We never sell or rent your data.
- We do not read your contacts, your whole photo library or your other apps: only the photos you choose are used.

## 3. What stays on your phone

Several operations take place only on your iPhone, without sending anything:

- removing the background of photos (Apple's built-in tool on the phone);
- detecting the colours of an item and giving photo tips;
- choosing the outfit of the day (colour-matching rules);
- the morning reminder (a notification scheduled on the phone, with no server involved);
- a copy of your wardrobe, for instant display, and your display settings.

## 4. Why we use this data, and on what legal basis

- **Running Vestia** (account, wardrobe, photos, outfits, translations, automatic sorting into albums, syncing between your devices, downloading your data): performance of the contract between us, namely the Terms of Use (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- **Showing the weather** based on your location: your consent, given through the iPhone permission (Article 6(1)(a)). You may withdraw it at any time in the iPhone's Settings, Vestia, Location.
- **Using the camera, your photos and notifications**: your consent, given through the iPhone permissions, which you may withdraw in the same way.
- **Security, abuse prevention and cost control** (technical logs, number of artificial intelligence requests): our legitimate interest in protecting the service (Article 6(1)(f)).

Vestia makes no decision producing legal effects concerning you based solely on automated processing.

## 5. Services that receive data (processors)

We rely on a few providers, who may use your data only to provide their service to us:

- **Supabase** (hosting of the account, the database and the photos). Servers in Ireland, within the European Union. Supabase is an American company.
- **Anthropic** (Claude artificial intelligence), United States. It receives the name, brand and shade of your items, in order to translate them into the app language and sort them into albums. It receives neither your email nor your first name. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic does not use this data to train its models and keeps it only for a limited period.
- **Resend** (sending sign-in emails), United States. It receives your email address.
- **Open-Meteo** (weather), OpenMeteo GmbH, Switzerland. It receives the rounded location (or the city you typed) and the phone's IP address, with no link to your account. Open-Meteo acts as an independent controller and deletes its logs after 90 days.
- **Apple**: background removal and notifications take place on the phone using Apple's tools, with no data sent by Vestia. The name of the weather city is found by Apple's location service, under Apple's privacy policy.

Exchange rates are fetched daily by our server from ExchangeRate-API, without any data about you.

## 6. Transfers outside the European Union

Some providers are based in the United States (Anthropic, Resend, and the company Supabase). These transfers are governed by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses included in their data processing agreements and, where the provider participates in it, by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. Switzerland (Open-Meteo) benefits from an adequacy decision of the European Commission.

## 7. How long we keep data

- **Account, wardrobe, photos, outfit journal and preferences**: for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, everything is erased immediately from our database and our photo storage, along with the copy of your account on that phone.
- **Removed items**: they remain on your account (archived), so that your past outfits stay complete, until the account is deleted.
- **Number and cost of artificial intelligence requests**: when the account is deleted, these records are no longer linked to you. They become anonymous and are used only to monitor our costs.
- **Our host's technical logs**: a few days.
- **Data kept by Anthropic**: the limited period set out in its terms, 30 days at most, unless required by law.
- **Open-Meteo logs**: 90 days, under its terms.

## 8. Your rights

You have the following rights over your data:

- **Access and portability**: Profile, My data, "Download my data". You receive a file with all the data in your account and links to your photos.
- **Rectification**: you can edit your items, your first name and your preferences directly in the app.
- **Erasure**: Profile, "Delete my account". Deletion is immediate and permanent.
- **Objection and restriction**: you may object to processing based on our legitimate interest, or ask for it to be restricted, by writing to us.
- **Withdrawing consent**: at any time, in the iPhone's settings (location, camera, photos, notifications). Withdrawal does not affect what was done before.

For any request: contactervestia@gmail.com. We reply within one month.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Portuguese supervisory authority, the **CNPD** (Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados, www.cnpd.pt), or with the authority of the European Union country where you live.

## 9. Security

Exchanges between the app and our servers are encrypted (HTTPS). Each account can read only its own data. Your photos are stored in a private space and are accessible only through temporary links.

Please avoid photographing other people or personal documents: Vestia is designed for your clothes.

## 10. Minimum age

Vestia is intended for people aged **16 or over**. If you learn that someone younger has given us data, please write to us and we will delete their account.

## 11. Changes

We may update this policy, for instance when new features arrive. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version. If there is a significant change, you will be informed in the app before it takes effect.
